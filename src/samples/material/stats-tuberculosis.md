## Limitations

First, since the data is from an observational study, causality cannot be inferred. The relationships found, such as the relationship between detection and mortality, may be confounded by external factors such as HIV prevalence or socioeconomic conditions. Thus, our findings should be interpreted as correlations only.

Moreover, some observations are excluded in analysis due to missing values, which may introduce bias if some regions are more prone to missing data.
