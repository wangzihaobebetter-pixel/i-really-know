# Nursing care plan: congestive heart failure exacerbation

**Course:** NURS 412 — Adult Medical-Surgical Nursing II  
**Patient:** Ms. J, 72-year-old female admitted with acute decompensated HFrEF (LVEF 28%)  
**Care plan date:** 9 March 2026

## Patient assessment

Ms. J is a 72-year-old woman with HFrEF (LVEF 28% on echo three months ago), hypertension, and type 2 diabetes, admitted with 4 days of progressive dyspnoea on exertion, orthopnoea requiring three pillows, bilateral lower-extremity oedema, and a 2.3 kg weight gain. She admits to dietary non-adherence ("I had a slice of my birthday cake"). Vitals on admission: BP 152/88, HR 102 and irregularly irregular, RR 24, SpO₂ 91% on 2 L NC, T 37.1 °C. Lungs: bibasilar crackles one-third up. Cardiac: S3 gallop, II/VI holosystolic murmur at the apex radiating to the axilla. Extremities: 3+ pitting oedema to mid-calf bilaterally. BNP 1240 pg/mL; creatinine 1.4 mg/dL (baseline 1.1); sodium 133.

## Nursing diagnoses

1. **Excess fluid volume** related to decreased cardiac output and sodium/fluid non-adherence, as evidenced by 3+ pitting oedema, 2.3 kg weight gain, bibasilar crackles, and BNP 1240.
2. **Impaired gas exchange** related to pulmonary congestion, as evidenced by SpO₂ 91% on room air, RR 24, and orthopnoea.
3. **Activity intolerance** related to imbalance between oxygen supply and demand during ADLs.
4. **Deficient knowledge** regarding sodium and fluid restriction, related to dietary non-adherence.

## Goals

- By discharge, patient will demonstrate weight within 1 kg of dry weight and resolution of 3+ pitting oedema to trace or 1+.
- Within 24 hours of starting supplemental oxygen and diuresis, SpO₂ ≥ 94% on room air and RR ≤ 20.
- By discharge, patient will verbalise a sodium-restriction plan (≤ 2 g/day) and a daily weight-monitoring plan, and will state when to call the clinic (≥ 1 kg in 24 h or ≥ 2 kg in 3 days).

## Interventions and rationale

- **Daily morning weight, strict I&O.** Rationale: weight is the most sensitive early marker of fluid retention; ≥ 0.9 kg overnight or ≥ 2.3 kg in 3 days triggers a provider call.
- **Furosemide 40 mg IV BID with daily BMP.** Rationale: preload reduction improves pulmonary congestion; aggressive diuresis risks hypokalaemia and AKI.
- **Sodium restriction 2 g/day, fluid restriction 1.5 L/day.** Rationale: negative sodium balance drives diuresis; fluid restriction protects against worsening hyponatraemia (Na 133 on admission).
- **Supplemental O₂ titrated to SpO₂ ≥ 94%.** Rationale: improves alveolar oxygenation and reduces work of breathing; titrate down as tolerated.
- **Cardiac telemetry throughout admission.** Rationale: atrial fibrillation with rapid ventricular response and a low EF put her at further arrhythmia risk.
- **Patient teaching with teach-back at each shift.** Rationale: written and verbal discharge instructions have higher retention than verbal alone.

## Evaluation criteria

- Weight and oedema goal met by discharge; if not, follow-up weight clinic within 7 days.
- SpO₂ ≥ 94% on room air sustained by 24 h.
- Teach-back on sodium/fluid restriction and weight-monitoring passes before discharge; if not, education is repeated and discharge is delayed until teach-back passes.
